Jarnival Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026
Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Jarnival mobile game ("the Game") processes, why, and what choices you have. The Game is published by SerenovaTech.

1. Who we are

Developer / publisher: SerenovaTech (individual developer account owned by Şenol Tahmaz, Turkey).
Contact: topalfatih7@gmail.com

SerenovaTech is the data controller for the Game under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, where applicable, the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Summary

  • No account, no sign-up, and no servers or databases of ours.
  • We (the developer) do not collect personal data.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob (rewarded and interstitial ads). Google may collect device data for ads, as described below.
  • An optional one-time “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Google Play. We never receive your card details.

3. Data stored on your device

Game progress (levels, stars, coins, helpers, collection items, badges), the nickname and avatar you choose, settings (sound, music, language, vibration), and an “ads removed” flag if you bought Remove Ads. This data is saved in the app's private storage on your device and is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

4. Advertising – Google AdMob

The Game is free and shows Google AdMob ads: rewarded ads (optional, you choose to watch them for a small in-game reward) and interstitial ads (between levels). To serve ads, Google and its ad partners may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • usage and ad interaction data (ad requests, impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data.

This is used for showing ads, frequency capping, ad measurement and reporting, fraud and abuse prevention and, only if you consent, personalized ads.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. In-app purchase – Remove Ads

The Game offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase, “Remove Ads”, which turns off interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads stay available. The purchase is made and processed entirely by Google Play (Google Play's billing system) under Google's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store your card or payment details. Google Play tells the Game only whether you own the purchase, and the Game then stores an “ads removed” flag locally on your device. We keep no purchase records on any server. Your purchase history is kept by Google and is linked to your Google account; you can restore the purchase on a new device with “Restore purchases” in Settings. Google Payments privacy notice: payments.google.com.

6. Consent and your choices

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (and elsewhere where required by law) are asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalized ads are shown. You can accept, decline (“Do not consent”), or manage options. If you decline, the Game still works and you see non-personalized ads, which are based on context rather than your profile.

You can change your choice at any time in the Game under Settings > Privacy / consent options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads / Google > Ads).

7. Legal bases (GDPR / KVKK)

  • Personalized ads and storing/accessing information on your device: your consent.
  • Non-personalized ads, security and fraud prevention: legitimate interests.
  • Providing and restoring the Remove Ads purchase: performance of a contract with you.

8. International transfers and retention

Google may process ad data outside Turkey and the EU (for example in the United States) under its own safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses. We keep no data on servers; on-device data stays until you uninstall the Game. Retention of ad data is governed by Google's policies.

9. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not designed for or directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (KVKK Article 11, GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold personal data on servers, most requests are handled by changing your consent in Settings, resetting your advertising ID, or using Google's tools (https://myaccount.google.com). You can also contact us at topalfatih7@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Turkey: the Personal Data Protection Board, KVKK).

11. Security

On-device data is protected by Android's app sandbox. Ads may link to other websites or apps; their privacy practices are their own.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. If we add features that change how data is handled, this policy will be updated first.

13. Contact

SerenovaTech – topalfatih7@gmail.com

Yeni bir cihazda Ayarlar'daki “Satın alımı geri yükle” ile satın alımınızı geri yükleyebilirsiniz. Google Payments gizlilik bildirimi: payments.google.com.
